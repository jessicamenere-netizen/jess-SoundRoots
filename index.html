SOUND ROOTS

Where Nature Becomes Music. An interactive environmental sculpture translating air quality into immersive light and Sound experiences.

About The Installation

Sound Roots reveals hidden environmental conditions through sensory experiences. Using embedded sensors, the sculpture continuously measures air quality, transforming data into coloured light and alerting Sound.

How To Read The Sculpture

🟢 Green Light

Healthy air quality with low pollution levels.

🟡 Yellow Light

Moderate air quality requiring awareness and monitoring.

🔴 Red Light

Poor air quality indicating increased environmental stress.

What The Music Means

🎷 Upbeat, postive Sound represents healthy, thriving plant life.
🎶 Silence indicates moderate air levels.
🎺 Intense, alerting Sound signals dry soil and environmental stress.

Why It Matters

Healthy air supports biodiversity, plant growth and carbon storage.
Clean air improves human health and environmental resilience.
Environmental awareness inspires sustainable action.
“Nature is always communicating. Sound Roots simply helps us listen.”
